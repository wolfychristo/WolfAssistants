# 🎯 Wolfy Pricing Strategy - 2024

## Executive Summary

This document outlines a comprehensive pricing strategy for Wolfy, our AI-powered email automation SaaS platform. The strategy balances competitive positioning, cost recovery, and value-based pricing while accommodating our current 100 free users.

## 💰 Cost Structure Analysis

### **Direct Costs per User (Estimated)**

| Cost Component | Monthly Cost | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|--------|
| **Gemini API** | $0.50 - $5.00 | Based on 1,000-10,000 requests |
| **Infrastructure** | $5.00 | Servers, database, storage |
| **Support** | $2.00 | Customer service, documentation |
| **Maintenance** | $3.00 | Development, updates, security |
| **Total Base Cost** | $10.50 - $15.00 | Per user monthly |

### **Break-Even Analysis**

- **Break-even at Starter Tier**: ~$5/month (very thin margin, relies on volume)
- **Break-even at Professional Tier**: ~$20/month (modest margin)
- **Break-even at Enterprise Tier**: ~$99/month provides healthy margins
- **Strategy**: Volume-based pricing, focus on user acquisition

## 🎯 Recommended Pricing Tiers

### **1. Free Tier (Current Beta Users)**
- **Price**: $0/month
- **Limits**: 20 contacts, 20 emails using Gemini/month, 50 Wolfy prompts/month
- **Features**: Basic email automation, rule-based responses
- **Target**: First 100 users, beta testers

### **2. Starter Tier**
- **Price**: $5/month
- **Limits**: 500 AI requests/month, 50 emails/day, 200 contacts
- **Features**:
  - AI-powered email generation
  - Basic conversation context
  - Contact management
  - Email scheduling
  - Basic analytics

### **3. Professional Tier**
- **Price**: $20/month
- **Limits**: 2,000 AI requests/month, 200 emails/day, 1,000 contacts
- **Features** (includes Starter +):
  - Priority AI processing
  - Advanced conversation memory
  - Team collaboration features
  - Advanced analytics and reporting
  - API access
  - Meeting scheduling automation

### **4. Enterprise Tier**
- **Price**: $99/month
- **Limits**: 10,000 AI requests/month, 1,000 emails/day, 5,000 contacts
- **Features** (includes Professional +):
  - Custom AI training
  - White-label options
  - Dedicated account manager
  - Custom integrations
  - Advanced security features
  - SLA guarantees

## 🎨 Value-Based Pricing Rationale

### **Why These Prices?**

1. **Aggressive Market Penetration**: $5 vs Mailchimp's $13 (dramatically undercut competition)
2. **AI Value Proposition**: Even at $5, we offer superior AI features vs $25 competitors
3. **Volume Strategy**: Thin margins compensated by rapid user acquisition
4. **Enterprise Upsell**: $99 creates strong incentive to upgrade from $20 tier

### **Price Psychology**

- **$5**: "No-brainer" impulse purchase price point
- **$20**: Premium feel but still accessible for growing businesses
- **$99**: Significant jump that justifies enterprise features and support

### **Strategic Positioning**

- **Penetration Pricing**: Deliberately low to capture market share quickly
- **AI Differentiation**: Even at low prices, AI features provide superior value
- **Volume Focus**: Profit through scale rather than high margins per user
- **Upgrade Path**: Clear progression from $5 → $20 → $99

## 📊 Usage-Based Pricing Alternative

### **Pay-Per-Use Option**

For very light users, consider:

| Metric | Price |
|--------|-------|
| **AI Requests** | $0.01 per request |
| **Email Sent** | $0.05 per email |
| **Contact Storage** | $0.01 per contact/month |

**Hybrid Model Recommendation:**
- Base subscription ($5/month) + usage fees for heavy users
- Maximum cap to prevent bill shock
- Simple tier upgrades when usage increases

### **Current Strategy: Penetration Pricing**
- **Focus**: Rapid user acquisition through aggressive pricing
- **Goal**: 1000+ users in first year
- **Scaling**: Plan price increases after reaching critical mass
- **Monitoring**: Track costs closely to ensure sustainability

## 💡 Risk Mitigation
